Getting started guide

Trial version

Jalonia · Version française · Open the application

In 15 minutes: look at an example, write your first document, get the Word or PDF file. The application runs in your browser (Chrome or Edge recommended): nothing is sent over the internet.

  1. Look at an example

    Menu File › Work instruction example (or Procedure example). Go through the 4 steps in the left column: Identification, Flow, Summary, Generation. Click Flowchart preview (step 2) to see the drawing.

  2. Describe your organisation (optional)

    File › Process map: import the PDF of your process map, enter your processes, or load “Example”. Identification then offers your processes, your area (Management, Realisation, Support, Monitoring & measurement) and your codes.

  3. Start your document

    File › New document, then choose Procedure (level 2) or Work instruction (level 3). Fill in step 1, Identification.

  4. Write the flow

    In step 2, one instruction at a time: a verb in the infinitive form, 5 words at most, one role, what comes in and what goes out. The pills add a specific feature (check, decision, delay, indicator…).

  5. Check

    The coloured bar at the bottom counts errors and warnings against the rules of the language (click it to expand). In step 3, review the summary then approve it: step 4 opens.

  6. Generate and save

    In step 4, download the Word file or save as PDF. Save button: a .json file to keep; Open a file to resume it. Your draft lives in the browser: clearing its data erases it.

Rules at a glance

  • Procedure: 6 roles at most; 5 to 10 instructions; 5 A4 pages at most; flowchart text readable (5.5 pt minimum).
  • Work instruction: a single role; 5 to 10 operations; each check is a question; one A4 page.

Stuck, got an idea, spotted a mistake in the drawing? Click Send feedback at the bottom of the left column: the version and your browser are prepared for you. Say what you wanted to do and what happened.
